Hey there,
I’m Orissa.

My work emulates the sun. I show up consistently, with presence, building ideas that shift the energy around them. I grew up South Asian, with a name rooted in strength and sunlight. Culture and heritage shaped how I see meaning, and that foundation still drives my work.

That instinct stayed with me. Over time, I saw design in everything. It does not need to be complex. Take something clear and push it further, make it sharper, more expressive, more alive. That is how I work now. I follow strong ideas and push them until they feel right, until they leave an impression.
